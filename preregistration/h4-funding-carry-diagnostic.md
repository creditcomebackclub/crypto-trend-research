# H4 — Funding carry diagnostic

Registered before any real-data strategy run. This is a diagnostic and cannot be promoted as a headline strategy.

- **Question:** Is delta-neutral long-spot/short-perpetual funding carry attractive after fees, funding sign changes, margin constraints, and tail events?
- **Universe:** BTC and ETH, plus SOL only if qualifying spot and perpetual histories overlap.
- **Position:** Long 1× spot notional and short 1× perpetual notional. No portfolio-level leverage is reported as a headline result.
- **Costs:** Entry and exit fees on both legs, observed funding with its published sign convention, volume-scaled slippage, and a conservative margin/liquidation stress model.
- **Primary metric:** Net annualized return and worst stress loss, reported diagnostically.
- **Success criterion:** None; this hypothesis is explanatory. It must state why the carry is or is not attractive and cannot be selected for promotion.
- **Forward start:** the first successful scheduled paper-trade workflow after the initial results release.
